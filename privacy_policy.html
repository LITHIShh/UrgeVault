Privacy Policy

QuitBet - Gambling & Addiction Blocker

Effective Date: October 2026

QuitBet ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data when you use QuitBet.

1. Data Collection & Processing

We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal information or browsing history. All filtering and blocking calculations take place 100% locally on your device.

2. Use of Android VpnService (Web Filter)

QuitBet uses Android's VpnService strictly to provide a local DNS sinkhole for blocking gambling websites and user-designated custom domains.

  • The VPN tunnel operates exclusively on your local device loopback (10.1.10.1).
  • Your web browsing traffic is NEVER redirected to remote proxy servers or logged by us.
  • Only DNS queries destined for gambling domains are dropped locally; safe queries are relayed directly to standard family DNS resolvers (e.g., Cloudflare Family DNS).

3. Use of Android AccessibilityService (Addiction Guardian)

QuitBet uses the Android AccessibilityService API strictly for self-exclusion and addiction recovery purposes:

  • To detect foreground package names and close gambling or user-blocked applications immediately upon launch.
  • To detect browser address bar URLs and prevent visiting gambling websites.
  • To prevent tampering or unauthorized uninstallation of the app without the user's master password.
  • We do NOT collect, read, or record user keystrokes, personal messages, passwords, or on-screen text.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

QuitBet is completely free to use and is supported by non-intrusive banner advertising via Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process standard device identifiers, advertising IDs (GAID), IP addresses, and diagnostic information to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements in compliance with Google's Play Services policies. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services at Google's Privacy & Terms.

5. Security of Master Password

Your master password / PIN is stored securely using a 16-byte cryptographic salt and iterated SHA-256 cryptographic hashing in private application storage. Your plain password is never recorded or sent off your device.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at: support@quitbet.app.
